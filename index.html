About us

关于澳洲江阴同乡会

中文

澳洲江阴同乡会成立于2019年，是在澳大利亚维多利亚州注册的非营利社团。协会致力于联系和服务在澳江阴籍乡亲、家属及关心江阴的各界朋友，通过社区联谊、文化交流、节庆活动与互助服务，增进乡情与友谊，促进江阴与澳大利亚之间的相互了解。

English

Australian Jiangyin Association Inc., established in 2019, is a not-for-profit incorporated association registered in Victoria, Australia. We connect and support people from Jiangyin, their families and friends through community gatherings, cultural exchange, festive events and mutual assistance, while fostering friendship and understanding between Jiangyin and Australia.
